# Changelog

## 0.2.0

First public release snapshot.

- Browse learned and unlearned paint recipes and count physical paint in your backpack and player boxes around your ship. Learn an eligible recipe with four matching cans and keep the cans.
- Gather a chosen color to a backpack target, gather all colors that fit, and return identifiable borrowed paint to its original boxes. Configure the two deposit options independently.
- Save and preview named ship and character paint schemes. Applying a scheme uses real cans from the backpack and eligible boxes, and returns removed paint to the backpack.
- Follow the game language or select English or Russian in Settings. Ship radius, favorites, interface preferences and presets persist across sessions.
